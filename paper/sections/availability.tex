\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\modelly{} gives Python modellers the agent-based programming model they know and removes
most of what it costs to do research with it. A model written once, with agents as objects,
runs its population-wide phases as array operations, 4.6 to 42 times faster than Mesa~2.3 and
2.9 to 33 times faster than Mesa~3.5 on eight workloads, and its counted populations two orders
of magnitude faster, while computing the same model: over 100 paired seeds no difference from
Mesa survives correction. Around that model, the steps of a study (sweeps, calibration,
sensitivity analysis, emulation, provenance and archiving) are part of the framework, built on
one seeding discipline and checked against problems whose answers are known, so a study's
numbers can be rerun and verified by anyone. The same model scales out to processes and GPUs,
connects to live data and continuous fields, and can be shared as a web page
(\cref{tab:scale}). For a researcher who needs many runs, large populations or results that
others can check, \modelly{} replaces the tools usually assembled by hand around a simulation
library with one package, one seed and one record. The conditions under which these results
hold, and the evidence still to gather, are set out in \cref{sec:threats}.

\section{Availability}
\label{sec:availability}

\cmd{pip install modelly-abm} installs \modelly{} (Python 3.12 or later), which is imported as
\code{modelly} and is free software under the Apache 2.0 licence. The release workflow builds
compiled wheels for Linux, macOS and Windows (x86-64 and ARM64) on the tag and a pure-Python
wheel for everything else (\cref{tab:determinism}). Source, issue tracker, the tests and the benchmark harness are at
\href{https://github.com/RemiPelloux/Modelly}{\nolinkurl{github.com/RemiPelloux/Modelly}};
documentation, tutorials and the roadmap at
\href{https://remipelloux.github.io/Modelly/}{\nolinkurl{remipelloux.github.io/Modelly}}.
At the tag, the suite runs 3,054 tests, of which 2,996 pass and 58 skip without an optional
library (CuPy, Ray, optuna, xarray, a broker) or a browser, with 97.3\% line coverage. The version described here is 1.4.2; the provenance of every measurement (commits,
machine, package versions and the Software Heritage identifier of the archived code) is in
\cref{app:reproduce}, and the data and scripts behind every table and figure of this paper
are in the repository's \code{paper/} folder. To cite \modelly{}, use the metadata in the
repository's \code{CITATION.cff}.
