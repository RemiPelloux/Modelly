## TechRxiv specifics

TechRxiv (https://www.techrxiv.org) asks, during submission, for: title, abstract, authors with
affiliation, keywords, a category (choose "Computing and Processing"), the PDF, a licence (CC BY
4.0) and confirmations that you have the right to post the work and that it is not
peer-reviewed. Answer the confirmations yourself.

## OSF Preprints specifics

OSF Preprints (https://osf.io/preprints/osf) asks for the PDF, title, abstract, subjects (from
its taxonomy, see above), licence (CC-By Attribution 4.0 International), keywords, a DOI if
already published (leave empty, or put the Zenodo DOI once it exists), and the conflict of
interest statement above.
