# Design reviews

A design review records, for one epic, what was decided, what else was considered and why the choice was made. The 1.3.0 roadmap asked for one page each for calibration (E1), sensitivity analysis (E2), Studio (E5) and language-model agents (E7); the Studio decision is in the [1.3.0 roadmap](https://remipelloux.github.io/Modelly/roadmap/1.3.0.html) (E5), the other three are written here, after the fact, against the code as shipped in 1.3 and extended in 1.4.
